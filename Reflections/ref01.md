# Reflection 1 in Module 1

During the first lecture, one of my key takeaways was the methodology used to teach the module. Rather than directly providing us with answers, the lecturer encouraged us to experiment with the tools and discover solutions independently. I found this approach interesting and engaging, as it allowed us to develop our understanding through practical experience rather than relying solely on theoretical explanations.

The best example is the 'SQL Island' where we are essentially learning SQL commands via a game. There were parts that were a bit more challanging, but that encouraged me and my team to try and work together to find the appropriate solution and eventually pass.

Going forward, I want to approach unfamiliar database concepts by continuing using experimentation and collaboration to develop my understanding before looking for ready-made solutions.
